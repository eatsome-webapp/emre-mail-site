Mailsome

Mailsome is a personal desktop mail client for macOS. It is used by its owner to read, sort and answer his own mail from several accounts, including Gmail.

What it does

  • Synchronises mail, folders and labels of the accounts its owner connects.
  • Sorts incoming mail, highlights what needs attention and prepares reply drafts.
  • Sends mail only when its owner sends it, or within automation rules he has explicitly activated.

All mail is stored locally on its owner's Mac. Mailsome is not offered to other users and has no server of its own.
